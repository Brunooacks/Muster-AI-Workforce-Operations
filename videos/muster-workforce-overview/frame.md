# Visual Direction

Enterprise editorial clarity. A light mineral canvas frames the current product
as evidence, while dark green typography and restrained lime accents create a
technological but welcoming tone. The video must feel like a guided operating
review, not a recording of screens.

## Composition Rules

- Keep the product capture as the dominant evidence in every feature scene.
- Pair each screen with one explanatory panel: context, decision and three proof points.
- Use cream and tinted white instead of pure white; use teal for structure and lime for progress.
- Preserve the current navigation, platform icons and light Mineral theme inside captures.
- Alternate left/right annotation panels to create rhythm without hiding the interface.
- Reserve amber and red for risk, review and SLA language already present in the product.

## Motion Rules

- Screens settle with 18–32px travel and slow camera movement between scale 1.01 and 1.045.
- Headlines reveal in two phases; labels and proof points snap into place afterward.
- Use a short fade through the canvas between scenes; never overlap competing screen text.
- Keep the final state readable for at least 2.4 seconds per feature.
